# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Codex power users who keep several locally authenticated ChatGPT accounts available and need to understand quota health, reset availability, and account readiness at a glance from the macOS menu bar.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable, non-disruptive control panel for monitoring quota, seeing when capacity returns, priming accounts, and switching accounts deliberately. Success means the user can identify the best account and any time-sensitive reset grants without opening several account settings pages.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, dependable. The interface should feel native to macOS, information-dense without being cramped, and candid about cached or unavailable data.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized pill clusters, repeated card chrome, low-contrast secondary text, hidden detail behind vague aggregate labels, decorative dashboard styling, and controls that compete visually with quota data.

## Design Principles

- Put time-sensitive information where it can be scanned first.
- Preserve detail instead of hiding it behind aggregate counts.
- Distinguish fresh, cached, unavailable, and exhausted states honestly.
- Keep common account comparisons compact and aligned.
- Use familiar macOS interaction and accessibility behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use system typography and semantic colors, maintain readable contrast in light and dark appearances, expose complete VoiceOver summaries, avoid color-only status communication, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
